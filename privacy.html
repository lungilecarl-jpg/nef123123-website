Privacy Policy

NEF123123 — Navigant Economic Freedom Website: https://www.nef123123.co.za/

NEF123123 (Navigant Economic Freedom) respects the privacy of every reader visiting https://www.nef123123.co.za/. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you browse our website, how advertising and analytics services operate, and how you can manage your choices.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse all public news articles and editorial pages on www.nef123123.co.za without creating an account or submitting personal details.

  • Direct Correspondence: If you email info@nef123123.co.za, we receive your email address and the content of your message solely to respond to your enquiry, editorial submission, or correction request.
  • Newsletter Updates: Live newsletter subscription storage is currently paused; submitting the homepage form does not store your email address in temporary server storage.
  • Optional Weather Location: If you click "My location" inside the Live Weather module, your browser asks for your explicit permission to read approximate coordinates strictly to display local weather. NEF123123 does not store your device coordinates on our servers.
  • Standard Server & Usage Logs: Like most websites, standard technical request data (such as browser type, device viewport, referring page, and aggregate page views) may be processed to maintain site security and reliability.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

NEF123123 uses Google AdSense to help fund independent digital publishing on www.nef123123.co.za:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to www.nef123123.co.za and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalised advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you may opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • For further details on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, visit Google's Privacy & Terms for Partner Sites.

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate readership trends—such as which articles and sections are most useful to readers—so we can improve navigation and editorial coverage. Google Analytics uses cookies to measure traffic anonymously. You can disable cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4. Affiliate & Third-Party External Links

Our website contains clearly disclosed sponsored placements and partner links (including Amazon and Shopify partner storefronts) as well as links to external news, social media, and public data sources. When you click an external link and leave www.nef123123.co.za, those third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and cookie practices. NEF123123 does not control third-party websites.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through browser preferences. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from reading NEF123123 news articles, though certain third-party ad or preference features may behave differently.

6. South African POPIA Rights & Contact

In accordance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal correspondence you have sent directly to NEF123123. To submit a privacy enquiry or request, please email info@nef123123.co.za.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, legal requirements, or third-party services change. Any revisions will be published on this page.
